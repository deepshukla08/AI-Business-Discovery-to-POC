# Zippo Logistics Pvt. Ltd.

## Standard Operating Procedure — Daily Dispatch

Document ref: ZL/SOP/DIS/04
Version: 1.2
Issued: 14 October 2022
Owner: Operations
Review cycle: Annual

---

### 1. Purpose

This document defines the standard procedure for daily dispatch operations at the Andheri East
hub. It applies to all operations staff, dispatch supervisors and delivery partners engaged by
Zippo Logistics.

### 2. Order intake

2.1 Client brands transmit the next day's delivery manifest by 18:00 on the preceding working
day, via email to operations@zippologistics.in.

2.2 The Operations Executive downloads the manifest and records all orders in the Master
Dispatch Sheet by 19:00.

2.3 Orders received after 18:00 are treated as exceptions and carried to the following day's
manifest.

### 3. Dispatch planning

3.1 Dispatch planning is completed the previous evening. The Dispatch Supervisor reviews the
Master Dispatch Sheet and allocates orders to delivery partners by delivery zone.

3.2 Zone allocation follows the standard six-zone map (Annexure A). No delivery partner should
be allocated orders across non-adjacent zones.

3.3 The completed allocation is circulated to delivery partners by 21:00 the previous evening so
that partners are aware of their route before reporting to the hub.

3.4 Each order is allocated to exactly one delivery partner. Reallocation after circulation
requires approval from the Operations Manager.

### 4. Morning briefing

4.1 Delivery partners report to the hub by 07:30.

4.2 The Dispatch Supervisor conducts a briefing at 07:45 covering the day's volume, priority
orders and any zone-specific instructions.

4.3 Consignments are handed over against signature in the Hub Handover Register.

### 5. Proof of delivery

5.1 On completion of each delivery, the delivery partner obtains the customer's signature in the
Delivery Register carried by the partner.

5.2 The Delivery Register is submitted at the hub at end of day and retained in the operations
filing cabinet for a period of twelve months.

5.3 In the event of a client dispute, the relevant Delivery Register page is retrieved and
shared with the client brand within two working days.

### 6. Cash on delivery

6.1 Cash collected against COD orders is deposited by the delivery partner at the hub at end of
day, against entry in the Cash Collection Register.

6.2 COD reconciliation against client brand statements is performed weekly, every Monday, by the
Accounts Executive.

6.3 Discrepancies exceeding INR 500 are escalated to the Operations Manager.

### 7. Failed deliveries

7.1 Where a delivery cannot be completed, the delivery partner records the reason code (Annexure
B) against the order and returns the consignment to the hub the same day.

7.2 Failed deliveries are re-attempted once. A second failure results in return to origin.

### 8. Reporting

8.1 A monthly performance report covering delivery volume, success rate and average delivery
time is prepared by the Operations Manager and shared with each client brand by the 5th of the
following month.

8.2 The report is compiled from the Master Dispatch Sheet.

### Annexure B — Failure reason codes

NA — Customer not available
AD — Address incorrect or incomplete
RF — Customer refused delivery
CD — COD amount not available with customer
OT — Other (specify in remarks)
